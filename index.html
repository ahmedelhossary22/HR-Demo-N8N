03 / HOW IT WORKS

Four n8n workflows behind the page

Each action on this page triggers its own workflow.

  1. Ask HR: chat workflow

    A chat webhook receives your question. An AI agent searches the HR policy knowledge base and replies with the answer and its source.

  2. Get coached: coaching workflow

    A public form sends your answers to an AI model that scores them and returns a structured coaching report.

  3. Apply: application workflow

    A public form collects the application, validates it and stores it for the HR team.

  4. Policy upload: admin workflow

    HR adds or updates policy documents in a private form. It is not exposed on this website.
